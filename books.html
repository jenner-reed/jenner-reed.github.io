Snowed Inn

A Winterhaven Holiday Romance

Welcome to Winterhaven, where everyone knows your business before you do. A solo winter getaway is exactly what overworked city girl Maxine Stewart needs to escape men and dating disasters; if only she could reach her destination.

When a freak snowstorm leaves her stranded in a village that takes holiday cheer to professional levels, she starts having very unprofessional thoughts about the local grump.

Tree farmer Brody Winter had his life figured out: keep his head down, take care of his community, and never risk the kind of love that destroys. Then a feisty little redhead twirls in like a candy-cane scented tornado, and suddenly, quiet nights alone by the fire seem incredibly overrated.
