Snowed Inn

A Winterhaven Holiday Romance

Maxine didn't plan on getting stranded in a magical small town with the one man who makes her want to commit arson and kiss him at the same time. But Winterhaven has plans of its own, and so does Brody Winter.

When a freak snowstorm traps her at the town's coziest inn, Maxine discovers that Winterhaven is full of surprises, and that the grumpy, impossibly handsome man running the place might just be the biggest one.
