The ARC Team

Read it first. Help it shine.

Want to read Snowed Inn early?

I'm building my ARC team for the re-release of Snowed Inn on July 25th, and I'd love to have you. ARC stands for Advance Reader Copy, which means you get the book before anyone else, totally free, in exchange for an honest review.

What you get

  • A free advance copy of Snowed Inn, delivered straight to your e-reader
  • First look before the official release
  • The warm fuzzies of helping a book launch into the world
  • A permanent spot in my heart (and my acknowledgments-section gratitude)

What I ask

  • Read the book before launch day (it's a cozy holiday romcom, so... not exactly homework)
  • Leave an honest review on Amazon and Goodreads during release week
  • That's it. Honest is the key word. I want real reviews, not just nice ones.

How it works: Sign up below to join the ARC Team. Closer to release, I'll email you a private download link through BookFunnel where you can grab the book in your preferred format. Easy.
