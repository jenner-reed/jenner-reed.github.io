About Jenner

The woman behind the words

Hey there, I'm Jenner.

Slinger of Banter and Spice. I write about strong opinionated women, swoon-worthy men, and all the chaos that happens when you put them in the same room. If that sounds like your kind of reading, you're in the right place.

I'm a small town girl living a city girl life, and when I'm not plotting ways I can turn my hotel job experience into future books, I'm dreaming up fictional men who would absolutely ruin my life in the best way possible.

The Writing Journey

I write what I like to read — laugh-out-loud romcoms one day, twisty, suspenseful slow-burns the next. What ties it all together is the butterflies, the tension, and a happily-ever-after that feels earned, no matter how dark the road to get there may be.

It starts with a single moment. One that won't let go. Then the characters move in, the banter starts, and before I know it, there's a whole world taking up space in my head that's demanding to get out.

If you're here, you're my kind of reader. This journey is just getting started, so stick around, because things are about to get good. Welcome to the chaos.
